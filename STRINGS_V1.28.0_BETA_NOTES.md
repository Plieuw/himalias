# Himalia Strings v1.28.0-beta

- Strongly reduced the initial bow scratch/click transient to 12% of its previous level for bowed articulations (88% reduction).
- Softened the bowed-string envelope attack rate to 65% of its previous rate for a gentler onset.
- Pizzicato and col legno transient levels are unchanged.
- Preserves the v1.27 output calibration, CV mapping, glide, ensemble behavior, and no-limiter/no-compressor design.

The purpose of this beta is specifically to reduce the initial plop without reducing the sustained note level. Please compare the first 100 ms of the note and the sustain separately. Hardware listening is still required.
