# Himalia Strings v1.29.0-beta

- Pizzicato ring-out extended by increasing the model's natural decay time; the initial pluck transient is not raised.
- Vibrato and tremolo rates now follow playing expression and phrase spacing rather than locking to BPM subdivisions.
- Legato and portamento glide is adaptive: interval size, note spacing, bow speed, pressure and dynamic level influence glide time.
- Detached articulations remain direct; connected notes retain continuous, unquantised pitch travel.
- Preserves the softened bowed onset from v1.28, CV mapping, ensemble behavior, output calibration, and no-limiter/no-compressor design.

Hardware listening is required to confirm the naturalness of the new response.
